How scanning works here

  1. 1Turn on the camera and allow access when your browser asks. On a phone it uses the rear camera automatically. No prompt? Tap Take a photo — device camera instead — it opens your phone's camera app directly.
  2. 2Frame the card inside the guide, in good light, and tap Take photo.
  3. 3Let it read the card. In-browser text recognition pulls the name, collector number and set size — the “4/102” that fingerprints the set — off the photo and matches the exact printing in the catalogue. If more than one printing fits, you pick from picture cards; it never guesses silently. The headline value is the raw (ungraded) price for that printing from PriceCharting, with TCGplayer market prices per finish alongside and live eBay listings.
  4. 4Add it in one tap. From a photo, the card page leads with a one-tap Add to collection — Raw by default, with Damaged and Graded (PSA / CGC / BGS / SGC / TAG) right there on the same sheet. A photo can't prove a 1st Edition stamp, so photo adds default to Unlimited unless you switch.

Live camera not prompting? Use Take a photo — device camera — it opens your phone's own camera app and needs no browser permission, and the photo works exactly the same way here. Upload a photo opens your camera roll or file picker. If you want the live viewfinder, open this link directly in Safari or Chrome (not inside another app's preview), tap the lock / site-settings icon in the address bar, set Camera to Allow, reload, and tap “Turn on camera” again.

Search the catalogue

Search pulls the card catalogue live (via TCGdex — the same TCGplayer data TCGCSV mirrors daily). The headline value on every card is the raw, ungraded price for that exact printing — set and number — from PriceCharting: Base Set, Base Set 2, Jungle, Fossil, Legendary Collection and 1st Edition vs Unlimited all price separately, because they trade at different money. Tap any card for the full breakdown, For sale on TCGplayer product cards per finish and live eBay listings in-app with Auction / Buy It Now filters, then tap through to buy.

About · Data & Partners

What this demo does, where its data comes from, and how buying works.

What it is

A trading card companion: photograph or search any Pokémon or Magic card, see what it's worth right now, track a collection's total value, and keep a wishlist with target prices — all in one place, on phone or desktop.

Data sources

Headline values are the raw (ungraded) price for the exact printing — name, set and collector number — from PriceCharting, fetched per card: a Base Set Raichu, a Fossil Raichu and a 1st Edition printing each carry their own value. Pokémon card images, sets and TCGplayer market prices come live from TCGdex, with the Pokémon TCG API as backup; those TCGplayer figures are near-mint market prices, shown as labelled per-finish rows (1st Edition and Unlimited separately), never as the headline. That TCGplayer pricing is the same catalogue TCGCSV mirrors as daily price files. Magic data and prices come from Scryfall. Prices are fetched fresh each time you search.

Buying

Every card page shows two ways to buy that exact printing. For sale on TCGplayer: one product card per finish (Holofoil, Normal, Reverse Holofoil…) with the TCGplayer product photo, “from” lowest current ask, market price and TCGplayer Direct price when available — filter by finish and sort by lowest ask or market price, then tap through to that finish's TCGplayer product page with every seller. Live on eBay: thumbnail, price + shipping and all-in total, bids and end time on auctions, seller and condition — fetched live via the eBay search proxy, with filters for Auction / Buy It Now and sorting by best match, price, or ending soonest. Purchases happen on the marketplace — this demo never handles payment.

For partners

Built by Muse Enterprise Solutions as a working product exhibit for marketplace and data partnership conversations — including TCGplayer affiliate (Impact) and partner data access. Photo identification (in-browser OCR of the printed name, number and set size) and graded-card states are live in this demo; the full version adds population data and daily price alerts.
