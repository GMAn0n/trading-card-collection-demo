Privacy Policy — Trading Card Collection

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Trading Card Collection (the “App”) is operated by Sam Clark (“we”, “us”). The App helps you scan, identify, value, and keep track of your trading card collection. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store your username and your password in hashed form (we never store your plain-text password). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and a unique identifier from that provider so we can sign you in.
  • Guest accounts. If you use the App as a guest, we issue a random token that is stored on your device (in the browser or app storage) and on our server in hashed form. It simply ties your collection to you when you return. It is not derived from your device, and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your collection data. The cards you add to your collection and wishlist, the details you record about them (such as condition, grade, and what you paid), and records of purchases you start from the App. This data is stored both on your device and on our server so it stays in sync across your devices.
  • Card photos you scan. When you scan or upload a photo of a card, the photo is sent to our servers so the App can identify the card. We do not sell your photos.

How we use information

  • To run the App: sign you in, keep your collection and wishlist synced, identify cards from photos, and show card values and marketplace listings.
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking, and we do not use device or IP-address fingerprinting.

Information shared with others

  • Sign-in providers. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, those providers process your sign-in under their own privacy policies.
  • Service providers. The App runs on hosting and database infrastructure (Cloudflare) that processes data on our behalf.
  • Marketplaces. Prices and listings come from third-party sources such as eBay, TCGplayer, and PriceCharting. When you tap a buy or listing link, you leave the App and that marketplace’s own privacy policy applies. Purchases happen on the marketplace, not in the App, and we never receive your payment card details.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Data retention and your choices

  • Your account and collection data are kept while your account is active.
  • You can remove individual cards and wishlist items in the App at any time; removals sync to our server.
  • To ask us to delete your account and its data, or to ask any question about your information, email us at 369samclarkisfine@gmail.com.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and sign-in tokens are stored on our server only as hashes.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this page, with the “last updated” date above.
